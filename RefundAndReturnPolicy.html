Overview

This document outlines the refund and return policy for our products. We aim to provide clear and concise guidelines to ensure a smooth return process for our customers.

Refunds (If Applicable)

  • Notification: Once your return is received and inspected, we will notify you via email regarding the status of your returned item, including whether your refund has been approved or rejected.
  • Approval Process: If your refund is approved, it will be processed automatically. A credit will be applied to your credit card or original method of payment within a specified number of days.

Returns

  • Return Period: Our return policy allows for returns within 15 days of the original purchase date. Unfortunately, we cannot offer refunds or exchanges after this period.
  • Eligibility Criteria: To be eligible for a return:
    • The item must be unused and in the same condition as received.
    • It must be in the original packaging.
  • Non-returnable Items: Certain goods are exempt from being returned, including:
    • Perishable items (food, flowers)
    • Newspapers and magazines
    • Intimate or sanitary goods
    • Hazardous materials
    • Flammable liquids or gases
  • Proof of Purchase: A receipt or proof of purchase is required to complete your return. Please do not send your purchase back to the manufacturer.

Exchanges (If Applicable)

  • Replacement Policy: We only replace items if they are defective or damaged.
  • Process: If you need to exchange an item for the same product, please contact us via email at info.fooddeliveryservice@gmail.com and send your item to:
    3, Ramadasar Street,
    Venkatesa Colony,
    Pollachi - 642001

Shipping

  • Shipping Time: Products are shipped within 24 hours using various carriers and our service boys.
  • Return Shipping Address: To return your product, please mail it to:
    3, Ramadasar Street,
    Venkatesa Colony,
    Pollachi - 642001
  • Shipping Costs: You will be responsible for paying your own shipping costs for returning items. Shipping costs are non-refundable. If you receive a refund, the cost of return shipping will be deducted from your refund.
  • Tracking: If you are returning an item valued over 100.00 INR, we recommend using a trackable shipping service or purchasing shipping insurance, as we cannot guarantee receipt of your returned item.

Contact Information

For any questions or concerns regarding your return or refund, please reach out to us via email at info.fooddeliveryservice@gmail.com.

Thank you for your understanding and cooperation. We appreciate your business!
