Introduction

This User Account Deletion Policy outlines the procedures and implications of deleting user accounts and the associated data within our platform. We are committed to ensuring transparency and user control over personal data.

1. User Account Deletion Process

  • Request for Deletion: Users can request the deletion of their accounts by contacting our support team via email at +91 8438604267 or through the account settings on our platform.
  • Verification: To ensure the security of user accounts, we will verify the identity of the requester. This may include answering security questions or providing verification codes sent to the registered email or phone number.
  • Confirmation: Once verified, we will send a confirmation email to the user regarding the request for account deletion. Users must confirm their request to proceed.
  • Data Deletion: Upon confirmation, we will begin the deletion process, which will result in the permanent removal of the account and all associated data from our servers within 30 days. Some data may be retained for legal compliance or as required by applicable laws, which will be handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy.

2. Implications of Account Deletion

  • Loss of Access: Once an account is deleted, the user will lose access to their account and any associated services, content, or data.
  • Permanent Deletion: Deleted accounts and associated data cannot be recovered. Users are advised to back up any necessary data before requesting deletion.
  • Loss of Subscriptions: Any active subscriptions will be canceled upon account deletion, and users will not receive any refunds for the remaining period.

3. User Rights

  • Right to Access: Users have the right to request access to their data prior to deletion.
  • Right to Rectification: Users can correct inaccurate or incomplete data before initiating the deletion process.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Users can withdraw consent for data processing at any time prior to account deletion.
